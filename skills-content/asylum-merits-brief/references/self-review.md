# Self-Review Checklist

Run this against the finished draft before delivering it. Fix what you can; flag the rest in the cover note. These are the things the firm's reviewer predictably catches.

## Blocking (fix before delivering)

- COMES NOW opening present (one paragraph, relief and grounds), and NO narrative "Introduction" section. The numbered argument begins at Section I (Statement of Facts).
- All nine numbered sections present, in order, with argumentative headings that state conclusions.
- Dedicated anti-circularity passage in the PSG section (Section III) engaging Gonzales-Veliz and Jaco (when any PSG is argued).
- Nexus argued under one central reason with mixed motives addressed; the government's recharacterization rebutted preemptively.
- Withholding and CAT argued independently; the brief expressly says CAT has no nexus requirement and survives rejection of every protected ground.
- The prima facie / hearing section (Section VIII) enumerates the testimony-dependent issues and preserves the pretermission objection.
- Conclusion contains the full cascade of alternative relief requests.
- Every fact in the argument sections traces to the Statement of Facts (Section I), and every fact there traces to the client's materials. Zero invented facts.
- Every citation either comes from the authority bank or is marked NEEDS VERIFICATION in the cover note. Zero invented citations.
- Client name spelled and ordered identically in the caption, the COMES NOW opening, the facts, the signature area, and the certificate of service. A-number, IJ name, and hearing date match the input everywhere.
- Internal section cross-references point to the correct renumbered sections.
- Cover page, repeated caption on the first text page, and certificate of service page all present.

## Should-fix

- COMES NOW opening is a plain statement of relief and grounds, not argument or a factual summary, and stays to one paragraph.
- Statement of Facts reads as chronological escalation; no analysis mixed in.
- Each analytical section closes with a short punch sentence; no more than one per section.
- Authority hierarchy respected on each contested doctrine (statute, regulation, BIA, Fifth Circuit).
- Relocation rebutted with specifics, not generalities.
- Humanitarian asylum fallback present where past persecution is severe.

## Surface

- No em dashes anywhere. Appositives set off with commas.
- No stray commas (watch lists and the pattern "X, and Y" where no clause follows; watch "no arrest, or protection" type errors).
- No footnotes. Citations inline and consistently formatted.
- Section symbols render correctly in the caption.
- Document is 10 to 16 pages, Times New Roman 12, double-spaced body.
