## 1. Filing location and receipt

- File with the court having the case; some hearing locations use a separate administrative control court. ICPM 2.1(a); 8 CFR 1003.31, 1003.13, 1003.11
- A document is filed only when RECEIVED; no mailbox or postmark rule. ICPM 2.1(a)(2)
- Court date stamp controls timeliness absent persuasive contrary evidence; courier or postal delay does not excuse lateness. ICPM 2.1(c), 2.1(c)(3)
- Full postage or correct courier label required; no postage-due items. ICPM 2.1(a)(3)
- No unrelated matters in the same envelope. ICPM 2.1(a)(5)
- No faxes. ICPM 2.1(a)(6)
- Conformed copy: extra copy marked "CONFORMED COPY; RETURN TO SENDER" plus self-addressed stamped envelope if a stamped copy is wanted. ICPM 2.1(f)

## 2. ECAS electronic filing

- E-filing through ECAS is MANDATORY for attorneys and accredited representatives of record, and for DHS, in every ECAS-eligible case; IJs may accept paper at their discretion. ICPM 2.1(a)(7)
- Paper EOIR-28 still required for non-attorney practitioners, motions to recalendar administratively closed proceedings, and disciplinary proceedings. Part I, 5.3(a)(1)(B)
- Unplanned ECAS outage on the last filing day extends the deadline to the first system-availability business day (EOIR Outage Log). ICPM 2.1(d)(3)
- Unexpected court closure extends a same-day deadline to the first business day the court reopens. ICPM 2.1(c)(2)(F)

## 3. Filing deadlines (call-up rules)

The IJ can always modify deadlines; a standing order or scheduling order in the case overrides these defaults. ICPM 2.1(b)

Master calendar hearings, ICPM 2.1(b)(1):
- Unrepresented non-detained: at least 15 days before the hearing if requesting a ruling at or before it; responses within 10 days of the original filing.
- Represented non-detained: EOIR-28 filed 15+ days before the master calendar vacates the hearing and triggers a scheduling order; written pleadings and removability evidence generally due 30 days from the vacated hearing date, responses within 20 days; applications for relief generally due 60 days from a removability finding. Missing an application deadline risks deemed abandonment.
- EOIR-28 filed under 15 days out: practitioner and respondent must appear.
- Detained: as the IJ specifies.

Individual calendar hearings, ICPM 2.1(b)(2). NOTE: the old 15-day rule is now 30 days:
- Non-detained: applications, amendments, supporting documents, and witness list updates due at least 30 CALENDAR DAYS before the hearing (rebuttal and impeachment exhibits excepted). Responses within 10 days.
- Detained: as the IJ specifies.

Day counting, ICPM 2.1(c)(1) and (c)(2):
- Day of the hearing or decision is day 0; count calendar days; deadline on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday rolls to the next business day.
- Motion for extension must be RECEIVED by the original deadline, is disfavored, does not extend anything until granted, and must state the due date, reasons, due diligence, opposing party position, and proposed revised deadline, with cover page "MOTION FOR EXTENSION." ICPM 2.1(c)(4)
- Asylum one-year deadline. INA 208(a)(2)(B); 8 CFR 1208.4(a)(2); ICPM 2.1(b)(3)
- BIA appeal due 30 calendar days after the IJ decision per ICPM 2.1(b)(5) and 8 CFR 1003.38, BUT verify 8 CFR 1003.38 on eCFR: the 2026 amendment sets 10 days generally with 30 days for qualifying asylum appeals. Flag the conflict and use the shorter period as the safe deadline.
- Specific deadlines are consolidated in Policy Manual Appendix C.

## 4. Motion-specific deadlines, limits, and contents

General requirements for all motions, ICPM 4.2:
- Cover page title accurately describing the motion (the court construes by content, not title). ICPM 4.2(b)
- Grounds stated with particularity plus the precise relief sought. ICPM 4.2(c)
- State the opposing party's position or the efforts made to ascertain it. ICPM 4.2(d)
- Statements of counsel are not evidence: attach affidavits, declarations, and documents. ICPM 4.2(e)
- Motion premised on eligibility for relief must attach a copy of the application and supporting documents. 8 CFR 1003.23(b)(3); ICPM 4.2(i)
- Include a PROPOSED ORDER for the IJ. ICPM 2.3(c)(1)(F)
- Once an appeal is filed with the BIA, the IJ loses jurisdiction; do not file motions with the court. ICPM 4.2(a)(3)
- Motion briefs optional; if filed, must accompany the motion and follow format and page-limit rules. ICPM 4.5; 8 CFR 1003.23(b)(1)(ii)

Motion to reopen, ICPM 4.7; 8 CFR 1003.23(b):
- 90 days from the final order; ONE motion. Exceptions: changed country conditions (asylum/withholding/CAT), joint motions, DHS motions in removal, battered spouse/child/parent, sua sponte.
- Must state NEW, material, previously unavailable facts supported by affidavits or other evidence; attach the IJ decision, the relief application if applicable, an EOIR-33/IC, and a proposed order. ICPM 2.3(c)(1)(D)
- No automatic stay of removal. 8 CFR 1003.23(b)(1)(v)
- EOIR-28 or EOIR-61 must accompany if represented or assisted. ICPM 4.7(b)(1)

Motion to reconsider, ICPM 4.8; 8 CFR 1003.23(b):
- 30 days from the final order; ONE motion; no reconsidering a denial of reconsideration.
- Must specify errors of fact or law with particularity, citing authority and the record. Attach the decision and a proposed order.

Motion to reopen in absentia order (removal), ICPM 4.9; INA 240(b)(5)(C); 8 CFR 1003.23(b)(4)(ii):
- 180 days if based on exceptional circumstances; ANY time if based on lack of notice or federal/state custody without fault; one motion; automatic stay while pending before the IJ.

Other motions, ICPM 4.10:
- Continue: written, detailed reasons with evidence, hearing date and time, preferred new dates; filing does not excuse appearance. ICPM 4.10(a)
- Change venue: good cause; next hearing date and time; fixed street address for the respondent; EOIR-33/IC if the address changed. 8 CFR 1003.20; ICPM 4.10(c)
- Recalendar (administratively closed case, not reopen): date and reason for closure, closure order attached if available, EOIR-33/IC; no time or number limits. ICPM 4.10(u)
- Amend a prior filing: cover page "MOTION TO AMEND"; does not affect deadlines. ICPM 4.10(v)
- Responses to motions: due within 10 days of the court receiving the motion unless the IJ says otherwise; silence risks the motion being deemed unopposed. ICPM 2.1(b)(4)

## 5. Cover page and caption

Every filing needs a cover page (Policy Manual Appendix D) with, ICPM 2.3(c)(6):
- Name and address of the filer (practitioner of record, pro se respondent, DHS, or EOIR-61 practitioner)
- Title of the filing (for example "RESPONDENT'S MOTION TO REOPEN")
- Full name of each covered alien exactly as on the charging document
- A-number of every covered alien
- Type of proceeding (removal, deportation, exclusion, bond)
- Date and time of the scheduled hearing
- Special circumstances flagged prominently, preferably highlighted top right: "DETAINED," "JOINT MOTION," "EMERGENCY MOTION"
- Amendments to a filed asylum application labeled, for example "AMENDMENT TO PREVIOUSLY FILED ASYLUM APPLICATION." ICPM 2.1(b)(3)(B)

## 6. Format

- 8.5 x 11 inch white opaque standard stock; legal size discouraged; smaller documents affixed or enlarged, larger reduced unless authenticity suffers. 8 CFR 1003.32(b); ICPM 2.3(c)(5)
- Dark ink, preferably black; briefs, motions, and supporting documents SINGLE-SIDED. ICPM 2.3(c)(5)
- Typed preferred; legible handwriting accepted; illegible filings rejected or excluded. ICPM 2.3(c)
- Times Roman 12 point preferred; double-spaced text, single-spaced footnotes. ICPM 2.3(c)(7)
- Consecutive pagination at bottom center or bottom right for ALL documents including exhibits. ICPM 2.3(c)(3)
- Binding: two-hole punch at top (2.75 inches apart, centered); staple top left or removable binder clip; no side or commercial binding; ACCO fasteners and paper clips discouraged. ICPM 2.3(c)(8)

## 7. Assembly order, copies, exhibits, tabs

- Application package order: EOIR-28/EOIR-61 (if required); cover page; fee receipt (stapled to application) or fee waiver request; application; proposed exhibits with table of contents; proof of service. ICPM 2.3(c)(1)(A). Parallel orders for other package types at 2.3(c)(1)(B) to (F).
- File only the ORIGINAL (one copy) unless the IJ instructs otherwise; consolidated cases need one per record of proceedings. ICPM 2.3(c)(2)
- Table of contents with page numbers whenever exhibits or supporting documents are filed. ICPM 2.3(c)(3); Appendix L
- Tabs: paper ROP cases use alphabetic tabs from "A" on the right side; eROP cases filing paper use separator sheets ("Tab A") instead. ICPM 2.3(c)(4)
- File photocopies, keep originals; originals available to DHS at master calendar and brought to individual hearings; state in the filing that originals are available. ICPM 2.3(d)(3)
- Photographs: name and A-number on the back of the original. ICPM 2.3(d)(4)
- Publications: precise title, date, page numbers; State Department reports must show the report year; internet material needs the full URL; highlight the pertinent passages and any reference to a party. ICPM 2.3(e)
- Criminal records: criminal history chart (arrest date, docket, charges, disposition, immigration consequences) with paginated attachments. 8 CFR 1003.41; ICPM 2.3(f); Appendix K
- Witness list: for each witness except the respondent give name, A-number if any, summary of expected testimony, estimated length, language, and a CV plus qualifications if an expert. ICPM 2.3(g)

## 8. Page limits and brief contents

- Pre-hearing briefs and motion briefs: body limited to 25 pages; motion to increase available; must be paginated. ICPM 3.18(b), 4.5
- Brief contents: concise statement of facts, issues, burden of proof, summary of argument, argument, short conclusion with the precise relief sought; signed by the pro se respondent, practitioner of record, or DHS. ICPM 3.18(c)

## 9. Signatures

- Every submission signed in ink or digitally/electronically by the preparer; typed or printed name under every signature. ICPM 2.3(b); 8 CFR 1003.102(j)(1)
- ECAS conformed signature allowed: /S/ Name. Signature stamps NOT acceptable otherwise. ICPM 2.3(b)(1)
- Only the practitioner of record signs; staff and paralegals may not sign submissions (they may sign a proof of service if authorized). ICPM 2.3(b)(2), (3)
- Law firms cannot appear; a filing without an individual practitioner name and signature is not accepted. Part I, 5.3(e)

## 10. Service and proof of service

- If all parties participate in ECAS, ECAS auto-serves, but the filing must STILL contain a certificate of service stating service was completed through ECAS. DHS is always an ECAS participant. ICPM 2.2(a)(1), 2.2(e)(1)
- Otherwise serve an identical copy on DHS (or the practitioner of record of a represented party) with a written proof of service. Exceptions: filings served during a hearing; joint motions. ICPM 2.2(a)(2), 2.2(e)
- Proof of service must state: name or title of the party served; complete address; date of service; means of service; the documents served; name and signature of the server. ICPM 2.2(e)(1); Appendix E
- Service by mail or courier is complete on DEPOSIT with the carrier (unlike filing). ICPM 2.2(c)
- A certificate of service printed on a form covers only that form; supporting documents need their own proof of service; the EOIR-28/61 proof of service does not cover accompanying documents. ICPM 2.2(e)(2), 2.2(g)
- Never serve the IJ or the court. ICPM 2.2(b)
- Place the proof of service at the BOTTOM of the package. ICPM 2.2(e)

## 11. Fees and fee waivers

VERIFY CURRENT AMOUNTS on https://www.justice.gov/eoir/eoir-forms before reporting. As of mid-2026 the motion to reopen or reconsider fee before the IJ was $1,065, paid in advance and electronically only through the EOIR Payment Portal (epay.eoir.justice.gov); no checks or money orders.

- The fee RECEIPT is filed with the motion or application (stapled to it). ICPM 2.4(a), (b); 4.2(f)
- Fee waiver: Form EOIR-26A filed with the motion or application; income and expense information; declaration under penalty of perjury of inability to pay. ICPM 2.4(c); 8 CFR 1003.24(d)
- Denied fee waiver: filing returned; 15 days to refile with the fee or a new waiver request; the deadline is tolled during the cure period. 8 CFR 1003.24(d)
- Missing, wrong, or uncollectible fee makes the filing defective. ICPM 2.4(f)
- Relief application fees are NOT paid with a motion based on eligibility; they are paid if the motion is granted. Visa petition fees go to DHS, never the court. ICPM 4.2(g), (h)
- One payment covers consolidated respondents. ICPM 2.4(e)

## 12. Translations

- Every foreign-language document needs an English translation plus a certificate of translation. 8 CFR 1003.33; ICPM 2.3(a)
- The certificate must be typed, signed by the translator, attached to the document, state that the translator is competent to translate the language and that the translation is true and accurate to the best of the translator's abilities, and include the translator's address and phone number. One certificate covering several documents must list them. ICPM 2.3(a); Appendix F
- An English declaration by a non-English-fluent declarant needs a certificate of INTERPRETATION: the document was read to the person in a language the person understands, the person understood it before signing, the interpreter is competent, and the interpretation was true and accurate. ICPM 2.3(a)

## 13. Entry of appearance (EOIR-28)

- Practitioner of record status requires a properly completed EOIR-28. 8 CFR 1003.17, 1292.4, 1292.5(a); Part I, 5.3(a)
- Attorneys and accredited representatives e-file the EOIR-28 through ECAS; paper EOIR-28 required for non-attorney practitioners, recalendar motions, and disciplinary proceedings; serve DHS with a paper copy when not e-filed. Part I, 5.3(a)(1)
- Scope box checked (all proceedings, custody/bond only, or all except custody/bond); changing scope needs a new EOIR-28. Part I, 5.3(a)(2)
- All bar admissions listed with numbers, including inactive or disciplined bars; the disciplinary box completed truthfully. Part I, 5.3(a)(3), (4)
- Appearance "on behalf of" the practitioner of record: on-the-record notice, paper EOIR-28 with the on-behalf-of box checked, service on DHS, IJ authorization. Part I, 5.3(g)
- The EOIR-28 or EOIR-61 goes at the FRONT of any package that requires one; motions to reopen, reconsider, and rescind in absentia orders must include one if the respondent is represented or was assisted. ICPM 2.3(c)(1); 4.7(b)(1), 4.8(b), 4.9(b)

## 14. Defective filings and untimeliness consequences

Rejection grounds (filing returned unadjudicated), ICPM 2.1(d)(1):
- Missing fee receipt or fee waiver request where a fee applies
- Missing proof of service
- Failure to meet language, signature, or format requirements
- Illegibility

Untimeliness consequences, ICPM 2.1(d)(2): late application deemed waived or abandoned; late motion denied; late exhibit excluded or discounted; late witness list may bar witnesses; late response leaves a motion unopposed. A motion to accept an untimely filing must explain the lateness and show good cause, preferably with affidavits. ICPM 2.1(d)(3)
