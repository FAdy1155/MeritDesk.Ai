## 1. Notice of Appeal (EOIR-26)

- Form EOIR-26 only; filed DIRECTLY with the Board, never with the court or DHS; a misdirected filing does not count. BIA PM 3.2(b), 3.4(b)(2); 8 CFR 1003.3(a)
- One EOIR-26 per person unless the IJ consolidated; original only. BIA PM 3.4(b)(3)
- A-number of every covered person; date and type of the IJ decision; type of proceeding. BIA PM 3.4(b)(4)
- Brief indicator box: if you check "will file a brief" and then file none without explanation, summary dismissal. BIA PM 3.4(b)(4)(C), 3.16(c); 8 CFR 1003.1(d)(2)(i)(E)
- Reasons for appeal stated with SPECIFICITY, identifying the challenged findings of fact and conclusions of law; vague generalities risk summary dismissal; extra sheets labeled with name and A-number. BIA PM 3.4(b)(4)(D), 3.16(b); 8 CFR 1003.3(b)
- Built-in certificate of service completed. BIA PM 3.4(c)
- Fee receipt or EOIR-26A fee waiver attached. BIA PM 3.4(d); 8 CFR 1003.8
- EOIR-27 (or EOIR-60) attached if a practitioner is involved. BIA PM 3.4(e)
- Original signature; copy of the decision being appealed included. BIA PM 2.3(d), 3.4(f)
- Never use the EOIR-26 to file a motion; never combine unrelated appeals (bond plus merits) on one form. BIA PM 3.4(b)(5)
- Paper package order: (1) fee receipt or EOIR-26A, (2) EOIR-26 with certificate of service, (3) EOIR-27 or EOIR-60, (4) supporting documents. BIA PM 2.3(c)(1)(A)

## 2. Appeal deadline

- Must be RECEIVED by the Board by the deadline; no mailbox rule; handing to USPS, a courier, or detention facility mail is not filing. BIA PM 3.5(a), (c); 8 CFR 1003.38(c)
- Deadline: VERIFY 8 CFR 1003.38 on eCFR as of the filing date. The manual said 30 calendar days from the IJ oral decision or mailing of a written decision; the 2026 regulation amendment set 10 calendar days generally, 30 for asylum adjudication appeals not denied under INA 208(a)(2)(A) to (C). Treat the shorter period as the safe deadline and flag the conflict.
- Day counting: decision date is day 0; calendar days; weekend or holiday deadline rolls to the next business day. BIA PM 2.1(b)
- NO extensions of the appeal deadline. Relief valves only: unplanned ECAS outage on the last day (BIA PM 3.5(b)(1); 8 CFR 1003.3(g)(2)); fee waiver denial 15-day cure with tolling (8 CFR 1003.8(a)(3)); equitable tolling via "MOTION TO ACCEPT LATE APPEAL" showing diligence and extraordinary circumstances, Matter of Morales-Morales, 28 I&N Dec. 714 (BIA 2023). BIA PM 3.5(b)
- A rejected appeal is not filed; the corrected filing is due by the ORIGINAL deadline. BIA PM 2.1(c)(2)

## 3. Fees and fee waivers

VERIFY CURRENT AMOUNTS at https://www.justice.gov/eoir/eoir-forms before reporting. As of mid-2026: EOIR-26 appeal $1,030 (no fee for bond appeals); BIA motion to reopen or reconsider $1,030; EOIR-29 $1,030; payment electronic only via the EOIR Payment Portal (epay.eoir.justice.gov), no checks or money orders.

- Pay in advance, exact amount; file the receipt with the filing. BIA PM 2.4(a), (b); 8 CFR 1003.8
- Wrong amount, missing receipt, or uncollectible payment: rejection or dismissal/denial. BIA PM 2.4(g)
- Fee waiver: EOIR-26A with the filing; declaration under penalty of perjury of economic hardship; denial triggers rejection with a 15-day tolled cure period. BIA PM 2.4(c); 8 CFR 1003.8(a)(3)
- No fee for: bond appeals; motions while an appeal or motion is pending; stay-only motions; DHS filings; joint motions. BIA PM 4.2(d); 8 CFR 1003.8(a)(2)
- One fee per consolidated group. BIA PM 2.4(e)
- Underlying relief application fees are paid on remand, not with the motion. BIA PM 2.4(i), 4.2(g)

## 4. Briefing schedules and extensions

The regime depends on the IJ decision date. BIA PM 3.7(a); 8 CFR 1003.3(c)(1):
- IJ decision BEFORE March 9, 2026: non-detained, 21 days per party sequentially; detained and cross-appeals, 21 days simultaneous.
- IJ decision ON OR AFTER March 9, 2026: 20 days, simultaneous, for all cases.
- Briefs must ARRIVE by the due date. BIA PM 3.2(e)
- Extension request RECEIVED by the original due date, labeled "BRIEFING EXTENSION REQUEST," stating due date, reason, diligence, and commitment to the revised date, with proof of service; a pending request extends nothing. Pre 3/9/2026 decisions: one 21-day extension possible. On/after: extensions only for exceptional circumstances per INA 240(e)(1), 20 days, once. BIA PM 3.7(c)
- Late brief: rejected; refile with "MOTION TO ACCEPT LATE-FILED BRIEF," brief attached, good cause or extraordinary circumstances with affidavits; one motion only. BIA PM 3.7(d), 2.1(c)(3)
- Not filing a promised brief: written "BRIEFING WAIVER" before the due date. BIA PM 3.7(e)
- Reply briefs: pre 3/9/2026 decisions only via "MOTION TO ACCEPT REPLY BRIEF" (surprise, within 21 days of the other brief); on/after, none unless the Board invites. BIA PM 3.6(h)
- New authority after briefing: "STATEMENT OF NEW LEGAL AUTHORITIES" (citations only, no argument); new argument needs "MOTION TO ACCEPT SUPPLEMENTAL BRIEF." BIA PM 3.6(g), 3.9

## 5. Brief and document format

- Page limit: brief or motion BODY limited to 30 pages. Counted: headings, footnotes, quotations, facts, issues, standard of review, summary, argument, conclusion. Not counted: cover page, tables of contents and citations, signature block, certificate of service, addendum of authorities, supporting documents. Oversized without a granted "MOTION TO EXTEND PAGE LIMIT" (extraordinary and compelling circumstances): rejected. BIA PM 2.3(c)(3), 3.6(b)
- Always paginated. BIA PM 3.6(b)
- 8.5 x 11 inch white opaque paper, single-sided, dark ink. BIA PM 2.3(c)(4); 8 CFR 1003.32(b)
- Times New Roman 12 point preferred; double-spaced text, single-spaced footnotes. BIA PM 2.3(c)(7)
- Cover page and caption on every motion, brief, and supplemental filing: filer name and address, title, each alien's full name as on the charging document, each A-number, proceeding type; "DETAINED," "EXPEDITE REQUEST," "JOINT MOTION" flagged top right. BIA PM 2.3(c)(6)
- A-number on the brief cover and bottom right of every later page; preparer signature plus EOIR ID number for registered practitioners. BIA PM 3.6(c)
- Signatures: handwritten ink on paper (no stamps or typed signatures); ECAS conformed signature "/S/ Name" allowed; typed or printed name under every signature; only the practitioner of record signs, never the firm. BIA PM 2.3(b); 8 CFR 1003.102(j)(1)
- Copies: original plus ONE set of supporting documents; separator pages ("Tab A") instead of index tabs; two-hole top punch appreciated; no commercial binding or binder clips. BIA PM 2.3(c)(2), (5), (8)
- Citations: "Matter of" with I&N Dec. for Board precedent; Bluebook otherwise; "Tr. at __" for transcript; "I.J. at __" for the IJ decision; factual assertions need record cites; NO new facts on appeal (Matter of Fedorenko, 19 I&N Dec. 57 (BIA 1984)). BIA PM 3.6(c), (d)
- Non-public source materials provided to the Board and opposing party, cites precise, passages highlighted. BIA PM 2.3(e)

## 6. Service and proof of service

- Every appeal, motion, and later filing needs proof of service on the opposing party (DHS; never the Board or the IJ). The Board REJECTS any submission filed without proof of service. Sole exception: joint motions. BIA PM 2.2(a), (d); 8 CFR 1003.2(g), 1003.3(a)
- Proof of service states: name or title of the party served; complete address; date; means of service; documents served; name and signature of the server (a designee may sign the proof of service only). BIA PM 2.2(d)
- ECAS all-party cases: ECAS notification is service, but a certificate stating service was completed through ECAS is still required. BIA PM 2.2(a)(1)
- The EOIR-27/EOIR-60 proof of service does not cover accompanying documents. BIA PM 2.2(f)

## 7. Motions to reopen (Board)

- 90 days after the final administrative decision, received not postmarked; ONE motion. BIA PM 4.6(c), (d); 8 CFR 1003.2(c)(2)
- State new, material, previously unavailable facts with affidavits or other evidence; attach the application for any relief sought. BIA PM 4.6(b); 8 CFR 1003.2(c)(1)
- Exceptions to time and number: changed country conditions (asylum/withholding/CAT); in absentia (goes to the IJ); joint motions; certain DHS motions; battered spouse/child/parent; sua sponte; ECAS outage; fee waiver cure. BIA PM 4.6(e)
- No automatic stay. BIA PM 4.6(i). Departure after filing constitutes withdrawal per 8 CFR 1003.2(d).
- Vacated conviction claims need clear evidence the conviction was actually disturbed. BIA PM 4.6(j)

## 8. Motions to reconsider (Board)

- 30 days after mailing of the Board decision; one per decision; no reconsideration of a reconsideration denial. BIA PM 4.7(c), (d); 8 CFR 1003.2(b)(2)
- Specify errors of fact or law with particularity, citing authority and the record; existing record only; cannot rest solely on objection to summary affirmance. BIA PM 4.7(e), (g); 8 CFR 1003.2(b)

## 9. General motion requirements

- No official form; NEVER the EOIR-26; written, signed, served; identify all covered persons; grounds and relief with particularity; the Board construes by content. BIA PM 4.2(b)
- Attach the Board or IJ order at issue. BIA PM 4.2(e)
- Statements of counsel are not evidence (Matter of Ramirez-Sanchez, 17 I&N Dec. 503 (BIA 1980)); all affidavits and documents go in WITH the motion, the Board will not wait for supplements. BIA PM 4.2(f), 4.6(f)
- PROPOSED ORDERS ARE PROHIBITED at the Board: "Parties should not include draft orders in the motion filing." BIA PM 4.2(j). (Opposite of the immigration court rule.)
- Disclose related judicial proceedings and pending criminal matters when discretionary relief is sought. 8 CFR 1003.2(e)
- EOIR-27 required with any motion by a represented non-DHS party; EOIR-60 where a pro se party got document assistance. 8 CFR 1003.2(g)(1)
- Opposition due 20 days from service, else deemed unopposed. 8 CFR 1003.2(g)(3)
- Compound motions: limits enforced per component (reopen/reconsider filed after 30 but within 90 days survives only as to reopening). BIA PM 4.3
- Motion to remand (appeal pending): substantive reopening standards apply (Matter of Coelho, 20 I&N Dec. 464 (BIA 1992)) but no time or number limits. BIA PM 4.8
- Motion package order: fee receipt or EOIR-26A; motion with cover page; supporting documents; EOIR-27 or EOIR-60; EOIR-33/BIA (recommended even if unchanged); proof of service. BIA PM 2.3(c)(1)(B)

## 10. Translations

- Any foreign-language document needs an English translation plus a certification signed by the translator, printed or typed, stating competence to translate the language and that the translation is true and accurate to the best of the translator's abilities. 8 CFR 1003.33; BIA PM 2.3(a); Appendix F

## 11. ECAS and paper filing

- ECAS e-filing MANDATORY for attorneys and accredited representatives of record, and DHS, in eligible cases. BIA PM 2.1(a)(6)
- E-filings are filed on the date submitted AND accepted; rejected e-filings are not filed. BIA PM 2.1(a)(1)
- Unplanned outage on the last day extends to the first available business day; planned outages extend nothing. BIA PM 2.1(b)(5)
- Paper filings: Board of Immigration Appeals, Clerk's Office, 5107 Leesburg Pike, Suite 200, Falls Church, VA 22041. No faxes without authorization; no CDs, DVDs, or thumb drives. BIA PM 2.1(a)(3), (5)

## 12. Entry of appearance (EOIR-27)

- EOIR-27 required per represented person with an appeal, motion to reopen, motion to reconsider, first appearance, address change, or record request; e-filed via ECAS by registered attorneys and accredited representatives; paper copy with paper filings. Part I, 5.3(b); 8 CFR 1003.38(g)
- Incomplete EOIR-27: appearance not recognized, filing may be rejected. Part I, 5.3(b)
- The EOIR-28 (court form) is NOT accepted at the Board. Part I, 5.3(b)
- EOIR-60 filed only together with the assisted documents; the party remains pro se. 8 CFR 1003.38(g)(2)

## 13. Consolidated filings

- Consolidation generally limited to immediate family and usually done below; Board consolidation via "REQUEST FOR CONSOLIDATION OF APPEALS" in each case. BIA PM 3.10(a)
- One consolidated brief only if the cases were actually consolidated; every full name and A-number on it; lead A-number plus "et al." as footer. BIA PM 3.6(e)
- Same legal issue, unrelated cases: "REQUEST FOR CONCURRENT CONSIDERATION OF APPEALS." BIA PM 3.10(b)

## 14. Evidence on appeal

- The Board reviews only evidence admitted below; new evidence on appeal is treated as a motion to remand. BIA PM 3.8(a), (b); 8 CFR 1003.1(d)(3)(iv)
- Administrative notice possible for commonly known facts (current events, State Department reports). BIA PM 3.8(c)

## 15. Defects, rejection, and summary dismissal

- Two most common rejection reasons: missing fee or fee waiver, and missing proof of service. BIA PM 2.1(c)(2)
- Other triggers: untimely appeal dismissed; untimely motion denied; untimely brief rejected; wrong fee amount; missing signature; oversized brief; missing or defective EOIR-27; wrong filing location; unpaid postage; unauthorized fax; missing certified translation. BIA PM 2.1(c), 2.3(b), 2.4(g), 3.6(b)
- Rejection does not extend deadlines; the correction is due by the ORIGINAL deadline. BIA PM 2.1(c)(2)
- Summary dismissal grounds, 8 CFR 1003.1(d)(2)(i): no specific reasons stated; conceded issue; appeal of an order granting the requested relief; improper purpose; no jurisdiction; untimely; waived appeal; failure to meet essential statutory or regulatory requirements; promised brief never filed. BIA PM 3.16

- Type: BIA precedent
- Text: (1) The respondent did not demonstrate that summary dismissal of the Department of Homeland Security's appeal was appropriate where he showed no prejudice from electronic service. (2) The Immigration Judge properly declined to consider documents filed after the merits hearing when the record was left open solely for the filing of other, specified documents and the respondent did not show prejudice from the exclusion of the late documents. (3) The respondent did not show he was prejudiced by the Immigration Judge qualifying the expert witness in certain areas but not others.
